# Privacy Policy for Nooji

**Effective Date:** September 9, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 9, 2026  

Welcome to **Nooji** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience when using our WhatsApp-based financial recording bot. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you explicitly and directly send to Nooji via WhatsApp messages. This includes:
* **Financial Transaction Data:** Transaction amounts, categories, dates, and optional notes sent to log income or expenses.
* **Basic WhatsApp Identifiers:** Your WhatsApp phone number or user ID, which is strictly used to associate financial records with your account.

We **do not** collect sensitive personal credentials, bank account details, credit card numbers, or any background device data.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Data

The information collected is used exclusively to deliver core features of the service, including:
* Processing and storing your financial transactions.
* Generating summaries, reports, and financial logs upon your request.
* Improving the overall functionality and responsiveness of Nooji.

**Data Sharing Policy:** Your data is strictly private. We **never** sell, trade, rent, or share your personal or financial information with third parties or external marketing services.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Security

* We implement standard security practices to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
* Data is stored securely and is only accessible by the automated bot process required to generate your financial logs.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You retain full control over your data:
* **Right to Access:** You can request a summary of your stored data at any time via the chat interface.
* **Right to Erasure:** You may request the complete deletion of your account and associated financial logs by sending a deletion command in the chat or contacting the administrator directly. Once requested, all your data will be permanently removed from our active database.
